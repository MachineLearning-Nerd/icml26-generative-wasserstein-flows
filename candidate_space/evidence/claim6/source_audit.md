# Claim 6 source audit

Proposition 6.3 states, under L2 Frechet regularity, square-integrability,
differentiable energy, and `Delta=O(tau)`, that

`G(theta_plus) Delta/tau = -grad energy(theta_plus)+o(1)`.

Cauchy-Schwarz. Invertibility of `G` is required for the equivalent
`G^-1`-preconditioned-flow interpretation.
