# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Paul Caucheteux, Clément Bonet, and Anna Korba for presenting the
Wasserstein/JKO connections, divergence extensions, optimization orders, and
parametric-flow assumptions clearly enough to audit claim by claim. The paper’s
structure made it possible to preserve verified mathematics while reporting the
two narrow source-level falsifications without overgeneralizing them.

This repository is an independent audit. It does not imply review, approval,
endorsement, or collaboration by the paper’s authors.
