# Claim 2 limitations and deviations

The coefficient and scaling certificate is exact, but it invokes Lemma F.2's
measure-differentiability and optimal-map result as an audited premise rather
than formalizing it in a proof assistant. The independent numerical route
uses a continuous diagonal-Gaussian submanifold and deterministic quadrature;
it corroborates KL, chi-squared, and Jensen–Shannon but is not the universal
proof by itself.
