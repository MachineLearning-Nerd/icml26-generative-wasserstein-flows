# Claim 1 limitations and deviations

The order algebra is machine-checked exactly, but the cited
measure-theoretic minimax and Monge-recovery theorems are audited premises,
not formalized inside Lean or Coq. The independent numerical check is
continuous, two-dimensional, and anisotropic, but uses KL/Gaussian structure;
it corroborates rather than proves the universal theorem.

The verdict applies to the unrestricted objectives in Proposition 3.1, not
to arbitrary finite neural generator/critic classes or an optimizer's ability
to reach the saddle point.
