# Claim 4 limitations

This result does not falsify the extension of GWF to IPMs, W1, or squared MMD.
It also does not dispute the paper's correct statement that fixed-kernel
Algorithm 1 evaluates its witness in closed form. It falsifies only the
imported conjunct attaching “does not require explicit discriminator
optimization” to the adaptive-kernel Equation-18 MMD-GAN.

The finite instance is deliberately minimal because it is a complete
counterexample to the structural quantifier, not empirical evidence for image
quality.
