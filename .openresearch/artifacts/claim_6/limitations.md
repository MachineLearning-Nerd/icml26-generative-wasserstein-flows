# Claim 6 limitations

The universal implication is supported by an independently reconstructed
symbolic derivation, not a proof-assistant formalization of Frechet
differentiability. The numerical checker is a continuous nonlinear
two-parameter family rather than a neural network.

Unlike the rejected baseline, the checker does not set `G=1`: its metric is
parameter-dependent, positive definite, and strongly off-diagonal. The
identity substitution is an explicit failing control.
