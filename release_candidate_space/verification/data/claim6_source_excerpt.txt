Paper: A Unifying View of Variational Generative Wasserstein Flows
Source: https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2605.31369
Proposition 6.3; Appendix G.3, Proposition G.4.

Let theta_{l+1} minimize
F(mu_theta) + (1/(2 tau)) ||F_theta-F_theta_l||^2_{L2(mu)}.
Assume:
1. theta -> F_theta is Frechet differentiable into L2(mu);
2. its Jacobian is square integrable in a neighborhood of theta_l;
3. ||theta_{l+1}-theta_l||_2 = O(tau);
4. theta -> F(mu_theta) is differentiable.

Then, as tau -> 0,
G(theta_{l+1})(theta_{l+1}-theta_l)/tau
  = -grad_theta F(mu_{theta_{l+1}}) + o(1),
where
G(theta)=integral (partial_theta F_theta)^T(partial_theta F_theta) dmu.

The proof expands
F_{theta_{l+1}}-F_{theta_l}
  = partial_theta F_{theta_{l+1}} Delta_l + r_l,
with ||r_l||_{L2(mu)}=o(||Delta_l||_2), applies Cauchy-Schwarz to the
Jacobian-remainder term, and uses ||Delta_l||=O(tau) to obtain
o(||Delta_l||)/tau=o(1).
